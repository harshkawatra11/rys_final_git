About the Conference

About the Conference

Global Panchayat 2.0 was a landmark school conference organised by Rajdhani Yuva Sansad at JKG International School, Indirapuram, bringing together students from Grades 4 to 12 in an immersive experience of diplomacy, governance, and global dialogue. With 8 dynamic committees, the conference provided young participants with a powerful platform to engage with real-world issues through structured debate, evidence-based argumentation, and collaborative policymaking.

The conference was designed to foster grassroots awareness combined with a global outlook — encouraging delegates to think critically about local realities while situating them within a larger international context. From junior Lok Sabha committees to advanced international forums, delegates of all ages had the opportunity to develop their voices, sharpen their skills, and broaden their perspectives.

— Inaugural Ceremony —

Inaugural Ceremony

The Inaugural Ceremony of Global Panchayat 2.0 was graced by the esteemed presence of Shri Ram Niwas Goel Ji, Former Speaker of the Delhi Vidhan Sabha, who served as Chief Guest. His address inspired participants to embrace dialogue as the cornerstone of democratic and civic life, underscoring the transformative role that young, engaged citizens can play in shaping a just and inclusive society.

The ceremony commenced with the lighting of the lamp and the traditional Saraswati Vandana, followed by a warm welcome address by the Principal, Smt. Nidhi Gaur, and an introduction by RYS Chairman Shri J.K. Gaur. The Co-Founders of Rajdhani Yuva Sansad then provided an inspiring overview of the organisation's mission and the vision behind Global Panchayat 2.0, setting the stage for two days of powerful discourse.

8 Committees

Committees & Their Scope

Lok Sabha Junior

Designed for younger delegates, this committee introduced participants to the parliamentary process in an accessible and engaging way. Delegates debated issues relevant to their age group — from education quality and school infrastructure to online safety — and learned the fundamentals of motions, amendments, and voting procedures.

The lively atmosphere encouraged even the shyest participants to find their voice, creating a memorable space where junior students discovered the power of structured, respectful dialogue.

The United Nations

The United Nations committee brought together delegates representing countries from across the globe to deliberate on pressing international challenges. Discussions centred on global cooperation frameworks, diplomacy in conflict zones, and sustainable development — encouraging participants to think beyond borders and develop empathy for diverse perspectives.

Delegates demonstrated commendable research, articulation, and negotiation skills, producing substantive position papers and model resolutions that reflected genuine engagement with the issues at hand.

Creative Press

The Creative Press committee gave aspiring journalists, writers, and visual communicators an exciting platform to cover Global Panchayat 2.0 from the inside. Participants took on roles of reporters, editors, photographers, and social media correspondents, producing real-time content about the conference's proceedings and key debates.

The committee developed critical media literacy skills and reinforced the importance of responsible, factual, and impactful reporting in a democratic society.

International Olympic Committee

The IOC committee explored the intersection of sports, politics, and global ethics. Delegates debated issues such as athlete welfare, doping regulations, the role of international sporting events in fostering diplomacy, and the inclusion of athletes from underrepresented nations and communities.

Discussions were energetic and well-informed, reflecting a strong understanding of the broader role of sport in shaping national identity, soft power, and youth development across the world.

UNHRC

The United Nations Human Rights Council committee engaged delegates in nuanced and often emotional discussions about some of the world's most pressing human rights challenges — from refugee crises and freedom of expression to child rights and climate justice. Participants navigated difficult ethical terrain with maturity and compassion.

The committee produced robust draft resolutions and created a space where different worldviews were heard and challenged, strengthening each delegate's capacity for ethical reasoning and cross-cultural dialogue.

Lok Sabha

The senior Lok Sabha committee provided advanced delegates with a rigorous simulation of the Indian parliamentary process. Participants engaged in Question Hour, introduced private member bills, participated in clause-by-clause debates, and cast votes on substantive legislative proposals — mirroring the complexity of real lawmaking.

Debates were sharp, evidence-driven, and often spirited, with delegates demonstrating a high level of preparedness and an impressive grasp of policy nuance, constitutional provisions, and legislative procedures.

Historic Stakeholders Meet

This unique committee transported delegates back to pivotal moments in history, asking them to assume the roles of key historical figures and stakeholders in watershed decisions. By engaging with the past, participants gained a deeper understanding of how decisions made in earlier eras continue to shape the present.

The committee sparked rich discussions on leadership, ethics, and the long-term consequences of political and social choices — giving delegates a profound sense of historical consciousness and its relevance to contemporary governance.

International Press

The International Press committee challenged delegates to adopt the perspective of global media correspondents covering multi-lateral negotiations and international summits. Participants crafted opinion pieces, conducted mock press conferences, and produced editorial content that required both analytical rigour and compelling communication.

The committee reinforced the vital role of a free and informed press in holding power to account, while sharpening delegates' skills in writing, public speaking, and critical analysis of world events.

— Valedictory Ceremony —

Valedictory Ceremony

The Valedictory Ceremony of Global Panchayat 2.0 was honoured by the presence of Mr. Shiv Vats as Guest of Honour — an eminent Academician and Content Creator whose words of wisdom and encouragement resonated deeply with delegates and faculty alike. His address celebrated the richness of the conference and urged young participants to carry the lessons of diplomacy and dialogue into every aspect of their lives.

The ceremony was further enlivened by a soulful musical performance, followed by a welcome address by Principal Smt.

Nidhi Gaur and heartfelt reflections by the RYS Co-Founders on the journey and impact of Global Panchayat 2.0. The event concluded with the felicitation of Executive Board members and the recognition of award-winning delegates in all committees, with the entire gathering celebrating the promise and potential of Global Panchayat's next generation of changemakers.
