About the Conference

Yuva Samvad 2026 — महिला सशक्तीकरण को समर्पित

On the occasion of Shaheedi Diwas of Shaheed Sardar Udham Singh Ji, the Haryana Vidhan Sabha, in association with Rajdhani Yuva Sansad, organised Yuva Samvad 2026 – महिला सशक्तीकरण को समर्पित on 31 July 2026, bringing together over 85 meticulously selected young participants from various states and cities across the country. Chosen through a rigorous two-stage screening process, the participants assembled at the Haryana Vidhan Sabha to engage in substantive policy deliberations on the agenda, "Role of Women-Led Development in Building Viksit Bharat 2047: From Grassroots Empowerment to Leadership, with Special Focus on the State of Haryana."

The conference was honoured by the esteemed presence of Shri Harvinder Kalyan Ji, Hon'ble Speaker, Haryana Vidhan Sabha, who addressed the participants and highlighted the indispensable role of women in nation-building and democratic governance. His insightful interaction with the participants reinforced the importance of informed youth participation, constitutional values, and inclusive leadership in achieving the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047. His words served as a source of inspiration for the young participants, encouraging them to contribute meaningfully towards public service and policy discourse.

Conceived as a platform for informed dialogue and democratic engagement, the conference witnessed intellectually enriching discussions, evidence-based deliberations, and thoughtful policy recommendations that underscored the importance of women-led development in shaping India's future. The quality of debate, research, and participation reflected the calibre of the selected participants and reaffirmed the potential of youth to contribute meaningfully to public policy discourse.

Held within the prestigious premises of the Haryana Vidhan Sabha, Yuva Samvad 2026 emerged as a resounding success, fostering constitutional values, leadership, and civic responsibility among young minds. The conference further strengthened the collaborative vision of the Haryana Vidhan Sabha and Rajdhani Yuva Sansad to create meaningful opportunities for youth participation in governance while nurturing the next generation of informed, responsible, and visionary leaders.

— Inaugural Ceremony —

Inaugural Ceremony

The inaugural ceremony was graced by the esteemed presence of Shri Harvinder Kalyan Ji, Hon'ble Speaker, Haryana Vidhan Sabha, whose presence lent immense significance to the occasion. The conference commenced with the traditional lamp-lighting ceremony, symbolising knowledge, wisdom, and the spirit of new beginnings.

The opening remarks by the Co-Founders of Rajdhani Yuva Sansad, Adv. Isha Kapoor and Adv. Jai Saini, set forth the vision and objectives of Yuva Samvad 2026 – महिला सशक्तीकरण को समर्पित. They emphasised the importance of creating meaningful platforms for youth engagement, informed policy discourse, and constitutional values, while highlighting the central role of women-led development in realising the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047.

Addressing the gathering as the Chief Guest, Shri Harvinder Kalyan Ji, Hon'ble Speaker, Haryana Vidhan Sabha, underscored the transformative role of women as catalysts of social, economic, and national progress. He encouraged the young participants to engage thoughtfully with public policy, uphold democratic ideals, and contribute meaningfully towards building an inclusive, progressive, and self-reliant India. His inspiring address formally inaugurated the conference and set the tone for a day of substantive deliberations and constructive dialogue.

Sessions & Agenda

Substantive Sessions Mirroring the Vidhan Sabha

Question Hour

The conference commenced with the Question Hour, initiating rigorous legislative scrutiny on the agenda, "Role of Women-Led Development in Building Viksit Bharat 2047: From Grassroots Empowerment to Leadership, with Special Focus on the State of Haryana." Drawing upon constitutional provisions, parliamentary committee recommendations, and contemporary policy challenges, participants critically examined measures to strengthen women's representation in governance and public decision-making. Deliberations centred on the implementation of the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam, increasing women's participation in the State Cabinet, statutory bodies, commissions, and public institutions, while also addressing the effective empowerment of elected women representatives in Panchayati Raj Institutions by eliminating practices such as "Sarpanch Pati" through stronger institutional support, leadership training, and administrative safeguards.

The House further deliberated on advancing women's economic empowerment and social security through higher female labour force participation, entrepreneurship, skill development, and improved access to formal employment. Discussions examined the need for safer workplaces through effective implementation of the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act, 2013, stronger institutional mechanisms to combat cybercrimes against women, and policies promoting women's inheritance rights in agricultural land, financial inclusion, Self-Help Groups, and market linkages through heritage, tourism, and value-addition initiatives. Participants also highlighted the importance of accessible public transport, childcare infrastructure, industry-oriented employment policies, and increasing women's leadership in engineering, public infrastructure, administration, and sports governance to create an enabling ecosystem for women-led development.

The session further explored the role of gender-responsive governance in achieving Viksit Bharat 2047, with deliberations extending to maternal health and nutrition, AI-enabled early warning systems for child malnutrition, institutional delivery and food security, and creating safe and inclusive environments for women across educational institutions, workplaces, and sporting ecosystems. Marked by well-researched interventions, evidence-based questioning, and constructive policy recommendations, the Question Hour demonstrated the participants' nuanced understanding of contemporary governance challenges and reaffirmed that meaningful women's empowerment must extend beyond welfare to leadership, decision-making, economic participation, and equal representation across every sphere of public life.

Discussion Hour

The Discussion Hour shifted the proceedings from legislative scrutiny to deliberative policymaking, enabling participants to articulate long-form perspectives on the future of women-led development in the context of Viksit Bharat 2047. Through persuasive speeches and thought-provoking interventions, participants examined the evolving role of women as leaders, innovators, entrepreneurs, policymakers, and changemakers, while reflecting on how Haryana can build an ecosystem that places women at the centre of economic and social transformation.

The debate extended beyond existing welfare initiatives to explore the deeper structural changes required for genuine empowerment. Participants deliberated on fostering women-led entrepreneurship, expanding access to leadership opportunities, strengthening mentorship networks, promoting financial independence, encouraging greater participation in emerging sectors and public life, and transforming societal attitudes that continue to limit women's aspirations. Several speakers highlighted the achievements of Haryana's women in sports, governance, education, and enterprise as evidence that, when provided with equal opportunities, women emerge as powerful drivers of inclusive growth and sustainable development.

The session concluded with a compelling exchange on redefining how progress should be measured in the journey towards women-led development. Rather than limiting success to numerical representation or the implementation of welfare schemes, participants advocated for an outcome-oriented approach that evaluates women's leadership, economic independence, decision-making authority, safety, and quality of participation across institutions. Characterised by eloquent speeches, nuanced policy analysis, and constructive recommendations, the Discussion Hour reaffirmed the importance of sustained dialogue in shaping a more equitable, inclusive, and progressive society.

— Valedictory Ceremony —

Valedictory Ceremony

The Valedictory Ceremony of Yuva Samvad 2026 was held in the presence of Shri Harvinder Kalyan Ji, Hon'ble Speaker, Haryana Vidhan Sabha, whose inspiring address encouraged the participants to uphold the ideals of democracy, leadership, and public service. His words served as a source of motivation, reinforcing the importance of informed youth participation in nation-building.

On this occasion, the winners were felicitated in recognition of their outstanding performance and exemplary contributions throughout the conference. The dedicated officers and staff of the Haryana Vidhan Sabha, along with the organising team of Rajdhani Yuva Sansad, were also honoured for their unwavering commitment and invaluable efforts in ensuring the successful conduct of the event. Their collective support formed the foundation of the conference's success.

Certificates were presented to all participants in recognition of their active engagement, dedication, and commitment to democratic values. The conclusion of Yuva Samvad 2026 marked not merely the end of a conference, but the beginning of a renewed journey of ideas, aspirations, and leadership, inspiring young minds to contribute meaningfully towards the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047.
