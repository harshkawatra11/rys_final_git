About the Conference

Democracy in Action at Salwan

As part of RYS's school outreach programme, a series of Mock Lok Sabha sessions were organised at Salwan Public School, Gurugram between 2019 and 2021. These in-school events brought the parliamentary simulation experience directly to students from Classes 9 to 12, bridging the gap between classroom civics and experiential learning.

These sessions helped establish RYS's footprint in Gurugram and built a community of trained young parliamentarians outside of the Delhi-Ghaziabad belt.

Key Highlights

What Made This Event Special

  • Mock Lok Sabha sessions for Classes 9–12 at Salwan Public School, Gurugram
  • Brought parliamentary simulation directly into the school environment
  • Students assumed roles of MPs, debating contemporary legislative issues
  • Established RYS presence in the Gurugram region
  • Multi-year engagement (2019–2021) building a consistent civic education programme
  • Part of RYS's mission to democratise parliamentary debate education across schools
