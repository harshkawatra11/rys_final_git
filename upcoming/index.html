3rd National Lawyers' Parliament Festival · November 21, 2026

Festival Highlights

Building on the success of our first two editions — the 1st National Lawyers' Parliament Festival and the 2nd National Lawyers' Parliament Festival — Lawyers for Justice, in association with Rajdhani Yuva Sansad, brings together practicing advocates and final-year law students from across the country to deliberate on the agenda, "One Nation, One Election" (एक देश, एक चुनाव) — a major proposed reform of India's electoral system, raising important questions of constitutional law, federalism, and democratic practice.
