Haryana Yuva Samwad 4.0 · First Week of November 2026

Conference Highlights

Haryana Yuva Samwad has always been a platform for young voices to engage, deliberate, and experience democracy beyond the classroom. Continuing this legacy, Haryana Yuva Samwad 4.0 brings together young minds from across the country for an engaging democratic experience, fostering meaningful dialogue, thoughtful deliberation, and confident leadership. This edition focuses on the transformative role of women in strengthening India's democratic institutions, encouraging participants to explore representation, participation, leadership, and the importance of ensuring that women have a meaningful voice in governance.

3rd National Lawyers' Parliament Festival · November 28, 2026

Festival Highlights

Building on the success of our first two editions — the 1st National Lawyers' Parliament Festival and the 2nd National Lawyers' Parliament Festival — Lawyers for Justice, in association with Rajdhani Yuva Sansad, brings together practicing advocates and final-year law students from across the country to deliberate on the agenda, "One Nation, One Election" (एक देश, एक चुनाव) — a major proposed reform of India's electoral system, raising important questions of constitutional law, federalism, and democratic practice.
