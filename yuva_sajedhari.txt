YUVA SAJHEDARI 1.0
==================
Organised by: Rajdhani Yuva Sansad (RYS)

DATE
----
August 9, 2024

PARTICIPANTS
------------
100 eager participants from classes 5 to 7.

OVERVIEW
--------
Yuva Sajhedari 1.0 was organized for 100 eager participants from classes 5 to 7.
This event provided a valuable opportunity for children to learn about the
fundamental concepts of Parliament, the United Nations, and the role of the
International Press.

ACTIVITIES
----------
Through engaging activities, discussions, and interactive sessions, the participants
were able to grasp the importance of governance and international cooperation in
shaping our world.

OBJECTIVE
---------
The program aimed to inspire a sense of civic responsibility and global awareness
among young learners, encouraging them to think critically about the issues that
affect their communities and the broader international landscape.

SOURCE
